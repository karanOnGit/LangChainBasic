ACME CORP - REMOTE WORK & SECURITY POLICIES (2026)

Section 1: Working Hours and Availability
All full-time employees are expected to maintain core availability between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their respective local time zones. Flexible scheduling is permitted for the remaining hours, provided that all weekly sprint goals and customer support SLAs are fulfilled.

Section 2: Equipment and Security Guidelines
1. Company-issued laptops must use full-disk encryption (FileVault / BitLocker) and enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all identity providers.
2. Direct connection to production databases from personal devices is strictly prohibited. All production access must route through the corporate Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) tunnel.
3. API keys and production secrets must never be committed to source code repositories. Secrets must be managed via HashiCorp Vault or AWS Secrets Manager.

Section 3: Generative AI and LLM Usage Policy
1. Employees may use approved Generative AI tools (including Groq-powered internal RAG portals) for coding assistance, document summarization, and draft writing.
2. Proprietary customer PII (Personally Identifiable Information) and confidential financial spreadsheets must be sanitized or masked before ingestion into third-party cloud models.
3. Internal RAG deployments using self-hosted or HIPAA/SOC-2 compliant endpoints (such as Enterprise Groq API or local FAISS/Chroma vector stores) are approved for internal knowledge retrieval.

Section 4: Expense and Travel Reimbursement
Remote employees are entitled to a monthly Home Office stipend of $150 USD for high-speed internet and ergonomic workspace supplies. Expense reports must be submitted via Concur before the 25th of each month.
